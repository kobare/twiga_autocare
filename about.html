Who We Are

Twiga Autocare Centre Limited is an independent automotive services company. Originally established in 2007 as Twiga Panel Beaters, we have since evolved into a full-service motor repair garage. Our headquarters and workshop are located on Busia Road, off Enterprise Road in the Industrial Area of Nairobi City County.

Driven by a commitment to excellence, we offer high-quality repairs and outstanding customer service grounded in honesty, integrity, and professional expertise. Our rapid growth over the years is a testament to our reliability and passion, but above all, our ability to make our customers feel at ease.

Our skilled staff have the training and experience to work on the most advanced vehicle models. We are constantly pushing boundaries to exceed expectations in all areas of service and client satisfaction.

Our Core Services Include:

  • Accident repairs on all types of vehicles
  • Auto refurbishment
  • Panel beating and spray painting
  • Cargo and bus body fabrication
  • Mechanical repairs and service
  • Auto electrical works

  • Mission
