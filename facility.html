Our Facility

Modern Auto Workshop

WorkshopFacility

Located at NCC Property No. 51, Busia Road, off Enterprise Road in Nairobi’s Industrial Area, Twiga Autocare Centre Ltd offers a state-of-the-art auto repair facility capable of holding 30–35 vehicles at any given time. We operate across two spacious go-downs, underscoring our large service capacity and efficient workflow.

Our workshop is equipped with modern tools and equipment, including advanced spray booths with drying and baking capabilities, polishing and buffing facilities, and a clear drainage system for environmental safety. We provide comprehensive services from accident repairs and panel beating to mechanical, electrical, and A/C repairs.

“Fully equipped, professionally staffed, and built for efficiency.”

Security is paramount — our premises feature 24-hour guard services, radio alarm systems, and CCTV surveillance. We are fully insured against fire, burglary, and work-related injuries, and offer a clean, well-organized working environment for both staff and clients.

Twiga Autocare is proud to be a member of KEMRA, AA appointed (2-spanner rated), certified by the Ministry of Roads and Transport, and compliant with NEMA environmental standards.
